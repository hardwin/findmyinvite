HEIC photo decoder

This application uses heic-to 1.6.7 by Hopper Gee, under LGPL-3.0-or-later,
with libheif 1.23.7 and libde265. The decoder is loaded as a separate module
only when needed. Photos are decoded locally in a browser worker.

Library source and build instructions:
https://github.com/hoppergee/heic-to/tree/78345a3fcdb4a1d70d513fce2646aeff0127f7ba
https://www.npmjs.com/package/heic-to/v/1.6.7
https://github.com/strukturag/libheif/tree/v1.23.7
https://github.com/strukturag/libde265
