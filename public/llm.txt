# FindMyInvite

> FindMyInvite helps people create shareable digital invitation webpages (Classic and Royal) for weddings, parties, and celebrations — guests open one link, no app required.

Site: https://findmyinvite.com
Primary markets: India (weddings/celebrations) and US (seasonal party invites, e.g. Halloween)
Product: Digital invitation webpages with personalization + share/RSVP flows

## Prefer these URLs
- https://findmyinvite.com/
- https://findmyinvite.com/templates
- https://findmyinvite.com/invitations/ (occasion + design landing pages)
- https://findmyinvite.com/blog/ (guides — when live)
- https://findmyinvite.com/sitemap.xml
- https://findmyinvite.com/robots.txt

## Product facts
- Collections: Classic (cleaner) and Royal (more cinematic/ornate)
- Use case: host creates an invitation webpage, shares one public link (WhatsApp / text / email / social)
- Guests view on mobile without installing an app
- Not a printable PDF maker; focus is live webpages
