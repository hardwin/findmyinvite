Privacy notice — launch draft

Launch preparation: business and support details are awaiting confirmation.

Invitation details can include names, event times, venue addresses, photos and messages. Public invitation links are intended for sharing: anyone who receives one may view and forward it. Do not include information you do not want guests to see.

Guest RSVP submissions include the name, attendance choice, guest count and any message or email provided in the form. Hosts can access responses through their private management link.

Version 1 does not require an account. The private management link grants access to manage an invitation; protect it like a password. Browser storage also retains local drafts and management access on your device.

The production architecture uses Vercel for hosting and uploaded media, and Supabase for invitation and RSVP records. Local demo drafts may still remain only in the browser until published.

Anonymous usage events (page path, time on page, viewport size, and a random session identifier) are stored so the operator can review storefront traffic. They do not include names, RSVP content, or management keys. The operator page is not linked from public pages.

The business contact, retention periods and privacy request process must be confirmed before public launch. This draft does not claim a completed deletion process or a guaranteed retention period.
