Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

Version 1 uses guest access. Keep your private management link safe: anyone with that link can manage your invitation. Share only the public invitation link with guests.

When is creating an invitation free?

The launch offer is scheduled from 14 September 2026 at 11:59 PM IST until 14 October 2026 at 11:59 PM IST. Both Classic and Royal designs are included during this window.

Will I be charged automatically?

No. The launch offer does not start a subscription, collect a payment card, or automatically bill you later. Regular listed prices are ₹1,199 for Classic and ₹1,499 for Royal.

Can I share my management link?

Keep the management link private. Use the separate public link to invite guests. Save a copy of your management link before changing browsers or devices.

Can I change my details?

Use your private management link to edit the invitation. Preview the updated details before sharing your public link.
