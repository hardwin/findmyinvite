## 1. Executive summary

FindMyInvite sits in a crowded India market between **cheap freemium webpage builders** (eInvit, SuPraKu, Invitra, CardShilp) and **premium cinematic studios** (Nimantran, Dreams Invite, NIMNTRN). Closest product peers for **animated invitation microsites** are Varumo, Lovlio (Royal Temple), Join Wedding (global), and SuPraKu. The largest **catalogue gap** vs peers is depth (FMI 13 vs Varumo 91 / Bigdates 500+ claimed / Invitra 36+) and **faith/regional coverage** (Nikah, Anand Karaj, Kanjivaram, Gujarati kankotri, Kerala kasavu).

## 5. Implications for expanding beyond 13 templates

1. **Close cultural gaps first (Classic):** Nikah/Mughal, Anand Karaj, Kanjivaram/South silk, Mandala/mehendi-night, Gujarati kankotri, Malayalam/Kerala cues.
2. **Raise Royal motion vocabulary:** Temple doors, curtain parting, elephant gates, lotus bloom, envelope/wax-seal (study Nimantran + Join Wedding + Paperless Post craft).
3. **Defend price with catalogue depth:** Mid-market rivals sell 36–91 themes under ₹1,500; FMI’s ₹1,199/₹1,499 needs either more templates or clearer cinematic superiority.
4. **Occasion breadth:** Birthday/anniversary/housewarming interactive patterns (Lovlio, Bigdates) for non-wedding Classic SKUs.
5. **Differentiate vs video marketplaces:** Wedeogram/Canva own MP4 shares; FMI wins on **hosted RSVP microsite** — keep messaging there.
