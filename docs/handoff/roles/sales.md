## Approved factual offer

“Create a customizable invitation using an available released design, share its public page, and view guest RSVPs using your private management key.”

Regular list prices are **Classic ₹1,199 / Royal ₹1,499**. Creation is intended to be free from **14 September 23:59 IST to 14 October 23:59 IST**, end exclusive. Confirm deployed availability before promising access. There is no automatic charge after the offer. Future paid purchasing is not yet available.

A host must keep the recovery key/file. Anyone with that key can manage the invitation and see responses. Lost keys cannot be recovered through an account or email in this version. Public invitation links are accessible to anyone who has the URL.

## Before, during and after launch

**Before:** rehearse a demo using an approved template and non-personal test content. Do not promise all 13 seeded designs: they were unpublished at handoff pending review. Do not use source-branded media in sales material without clearance.

**During:** accept interested prospects only through an approved contact process and after service confirmation. Route publication failures to support rather than suggesting payment, credentials or private keys in chat. Do not request a customer's recovery file to perform a sales demonstration.

**After:** record consented prospect needs, objections and the point where the journey failed. Send feature requests to Product, not commitments to customers. Keep RSVP guest lists out of the sales pipeline.

## Limits and measurement

Do not promise unlimited photos/responses, guaranteed availability, permanent hosting, personalized WhatsApp previews, translation completeness or account recovery. Four photo slots and a latest-1,000-response host view are current constraints.

Track qualified interest, completed onboarding and common objections using approved reporting. No historical revenue or conversion figure is verified. Read [status](../STATUS.md), [runbook](../RELEASE-RUNBOOK.md), [risks](../DECISIONS-AND-RISKS.md) and [role index](../index.md).
