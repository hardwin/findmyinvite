# CMO — v1.0 positioning and announcement control

**Launch target: 14 September 2026, 23:59 IST.** Announcements depend on verified service availability and the Release Manager's go-live confirmation, not the clock or the existence of a Vercel URL.

FindMyInvite's initial proposition is a customizable digital wedding invitation with a shareable page and guest RSVP collection. The scope is a free, guest-only release. There is no checkout, subscription, account system or automatic billing.

## Ownership

| Decision | Named owner | Approval evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public positioning and announcement | TO ASSIGN — CMO | Final approved copy and release confirmation |
| Media/design rights in campaigns | TO ASSIGN — rights reviewer | Asset-level clearance |
| Support destination and response capacity | TO ASSIGN — customer operations lead | Working contact and staffed rota |

## Before, during and after launch

**Before:** approve truthful copy and coordinate Marketing and Sales. Regular list prices remain Classic ₹1,199 and Royal ₹1,499. The intended free creation window is 14 September 23:59 IST through 14 October 23:59 IST, end exclusive; confirm deployed server configuration before stating these dates as active. Existing invitations do not become billable automatically.

All 13 catalog entries were unpublished at handoff. Do not advertise a count of available designs until the live catalog is approved and checked. Downloaded source media and embedded source branding remain unresolved rights/brand issues. Hiding a catalog item alone does not make directly reachable prototype media private.

**During:** release owned-channel announcements only after a usable custom-domain journey, real support details, approved catalog and operational cover are confirmed. Start with a controlled audience. If creation is unavailable, pause promotional traffic and use a factual status message.

**After:** collect consented qualitative feedback on template choice, editing clarity and recovery instructions. Review actual publications and support issues before paid acquisition. No marketing messages should be sent by an agent without explicit campaign authorization.

## Measurement and boundaries

There are no verified customer totals, acquisition results, revenue, testimonials or SEO rankings in this handoff. Define future acquisition-source attribution without placing management keys or guest email lists in analytics. Request aggregate reporting from Data Engineering; do not export RSVPs for promotion.

Success is qualified hosts completing and sharing invitations, not impressions alone. Escalate availability to the Release Manager, claim accuracy to Product, and rights/privacy issues to Risk and Security.
