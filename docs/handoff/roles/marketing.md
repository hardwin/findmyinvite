## Before, during and after launch

**Before:** check every claim against [current status](../STATUS.md). Use FindMyInvite branding. The downloaded videos can retain embedded source branding; changing surrounding words does not remove that fact or establish permission. Do not generate invented testimonials, customer counts, market leadership claims or fabricated urgency.

The offer is free eligible creation for 30 days from the revised launch; list prices are Classic ₹1,199 and Royal ₹1,499. The removed countdown/pause/schedule helper lines must stay removed. No automatic charge occurs when the offer ends, and future checkout is not ready.

Verify canonical domain, public support destination, page titles and campaign links on the final deployment. Invitation social previews currently use generic metadata; do not advertise personalized link cards. Guest and management pages should not be treated as SEO landing pages.

**During:** publish only explicitly authorized campaign messages. Share public URLs, never management URLs or recovery keys. Check that announced designs actually appear in the approved live catalog and creation works. If public availability fails, pause outbound promotion and coordinate a factual update.

**After:** summarize real campaign activity, acquisition sources where measured, qualified host feedback and support themes. Do not export RSVP email addresses into marketing lists. Propose privacy-reviewed analytics before adding trackers.

## CMS and release limits

Supabase is the operator CMS. Template metadata/SKUs/blog rows are data; invitation rendering remains versioned React code. Blog content is plain text; complete SEO rendering and variation controls are deferred. Preview and production share data resources, so do not experiment with publishing schedules on production rows.

Measure completed invitation creation where attribution is available; impressions and click counts alone do not prove product success. Read [risk register](../DECISIONS-AND-RISKS.md), [runbook](../RELEASE-RUNBOOK.md), [15 checks](../PREDEPLOY-15.md), and [handoff index](../index.md).
