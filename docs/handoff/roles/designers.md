## Implemented design scope

The demo contains eight Royal previews and five Classic designs. Detailed matching focused on Royal Prestige, Emerald Noir, Crimson Royale and Rose Gold Blush. Original fonts, images, videos and available music were reused; some source branding remains embedded in media. These files are not automatically cleared for public commercial use. All 13 catalog seeds were unpublished at the previous cloud check.

Runtime renderer IDs are fixed. Crimson Royale uses the legacy ID `ivory-elegance`; renaming the visible label does not justify changing persisted IDs. CMS variations exist as records but do not dynamically restyle the v1 renderer.

## Before release

- Compare approved designs at 390 × 844 and 1440 × 900: storefront cards, type wrapping, image crops, closed/open/revealed states and section hierarchy.
- Review keyboard opening, skip controls, reduced-motion behavior, scratch accessibility and music controls. Do not claim an accessibility certification from these spot checks.
- Check that long edited names, addresses and program entries remain readable and controls do not overlap invitation content.
- Verify free-offer wording uses the postponed 23:59 IST schedule where a time is shown. Removed countdown/pause lines must stay absent.
- Review empty catalog, upload failure, unavailable publishing and lost-key guidance as real user states, not just ideal screenshots.

## Handoff and post-launch

Give engineers a specific location, observed difference and acceptance example for each defect. Prioritize obstructed content and misleading interaction feedback over decorative adjustments tonight. Do not generate replacement source assets or expand the template collection without scope approval.
