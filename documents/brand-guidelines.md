# FindMyInvite shop chrome — editorial lock (2026-10-30)

Desktop shop (960px+) is unhurried and grown up. Quiet and expensive, not sparkly.

## Tokens

```
--paper: #FAF7F4;
--ink: #241F1C;
--muted: #4A433E;
--clay: #7A3D36;
--surface: #EFE8E2;
--accent: #5C322C;
--radius: 8px;
--panel-radius: 24px;
```

`--muted` and `--clay` hold at least 4.5:1 on paper. On ink, body uses `#D6CDC4`. No text below 14px. No shadows.

## Type

- Display: Cormorant Garamond 300, `clamp(46px, 6.6vw, 104px)`, line-height 1.0, letter-spacing -0.015em
- Body: Jost 300, 16px, line-height 1.65
- Meta: IBM Plex Mono 400, 14px (floor; spec sheet said 11px), uppercase, letter-spacing 0.06em
- At most one italic word per headline, in clay rose

## Rhythm

light (hero), light (categories), surface (Best Sellers), dark (New Arrivals), light (Wedding Websites), accent (Invitation Videos), dark footer (why, custom, quotes, offers, links).

Hairlines instead of boxes. No glass, gradients, glow, icon grids, emoji, or extra fonts.

## Identity sheet

The mascot / purple-gold board still governs the wordmark file. It does not colour the shop chrome.
