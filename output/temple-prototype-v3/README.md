## Cost and limitations

The three clips generated after locking the opening cost **$2.02 in xAI credits**. Including the earlier $0.72 opening made from the rejected overhead view, V3 video spending is **$2.74**. Image spending is separate on Replicate; its responses did not provide a monetary charge. No paid retakes were made within this three-clip run.

The opening retains an open rear pavilion and a continuing push, and the sampled groom reveal no longer contains the old smoke burst or a title-to-floor gold stream. Remaining defects include changing tower decorations during entry, a later-than-requested board arrival, and a name reveal driven more by camera movement than stroke tracing. This is the completed review prototype, not a claim that every requested motion or architectural detail matched the original.
