# V3 generated motion review

Review method: timestamped frame samples at 4 fps, with an additional 8 fps entry sequence from 1.25–2.75 seconds in clip 1. These observations do not constitute a numerical similarity score against the original film.

## Opening — clip 1, take 2

- Uses the user-locked aerial image 1, take 7, and the approved Ganesha endpoint.
- Advances from the elevated sunrise view and descends toward the existing central pavilion. The tank passes beneath the camera; the front pillars and roof pass around and above it.
- The rear remains open in the reviewed entry frames. No opaque back wall is seen disappearing as in V2.
- The approach accelerates, then the camera continues pushing toward Ganesha as the lettering and petal shower become larger.
- Residual inconsistency: the distant tower's floral decoration and some architectural details change through the approach and interior arrival. Text is less stable while small during entry, then becomes readable. The first distant idol is too small to certify exact identity continuity.

## Ganesha to invitation — clip 2, take 1

- Camera exits left, sending Ganesha toward screen right. Close columns cross the lens and reveal the board, with layered petals and moving reflections.
- The board becomes visible around 1.75 seconds and moves toward its settled composition around 2–2.5 seconds. This is later than the requested 1.15-second arrival; the model did not follow the intended speed ramp closely enough.
- Total generated duration is four seconds plus one encoded frame. No local speed adjustment or frozen hold was applied.

## Budget policy

Keep one newly generated take per clip in this run. No automatic regeneration to chase these remaining defects. Preserve all prior prototypes and produce a separate stitched candidate for review.

## Invitation to groom — clip 3, take 1

- Backward release from the board leads into a right turn through the columns and a moving reveal of the groom and metallic name.
- The large smoke burst and continuous title-to-floor gold stream seen in V2 are absent in the reviewed samples. Warm floor reflections remain.
- The name is already substantially formed as it enters the camera view. The result emphasizes camera parallax more than the requested animated stroke tracing; the large lettering briefly crosses close to the camera before settling.
- Dense drifting petals continue through the final composition, with slow camera movement.
